★ Questions

What parents ask us most.

What if my kid grows mid-season?

Bring the setup in and we'll re-fit them into the next size up. Included in every rental.

How long is the season?

From pickup through the posted spring return date. Exact dates are confirmed in your reservation email.

Do I need to know their boot size?

Nope. Give us your best guess online — we measure and fit properly at the appointment.

What if the gear gets damaged?

Normal wear from a winter of skiing is expected. Damage terms are spelled out in the rental agreement you sign at fitting.

Can I rent for more than one kid?

Yes — add a package per child and enter each kid's fitting details separately at checkout.

When do daily rentals open?

We're building them now. Join the notify list above and you'll hear the moment booking goes live.
